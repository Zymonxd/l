My Baptism
A New Beginning

📅 August 13, 2023 📍 Highlands Camp
Iba, Zambales

One of the greatest and most meaningful achievements I have ever experienced in my life was accepting my faith and undergoing baptism to become a born-again Christian.

This unforgettable milestone happened during a youth camp in Highlands Camp, Iba, Zambales. It transformed my perspective, strengthened my relationship with God, and gave me a clearer purpose in life.

My baptism symbolized a new beginning. It represented my decision to leave behind my old ways and embrace a life guided by faith, kindness, and hope.

Looking back, I can confidently say that this experience continues to inspire me to become a better version of myself every single day.

💡 Lesson Learned

True change begins within ourselves. Faith gives us the strength to become a better person.

The People I Guided and Had to Let Go

📅 2023 - 2025 📍 Our Church

One of the greatest challenges I experienced involved the disciples I mentored in our church. I spent almost two years teaching, guiding, and encouraging them to grow in their faith.

Seeing their growth and transformation became one of the proudest moments of my life.

Eventually, because of financial problems and challenges within our church, I lost contact with many of them. It was painful because they became part of my journey.

This experience taught me that leadership is measured not by how long people stay with us, but by the positive impact we leave in their lives.

💡 Lesson Learned

True leadership is helping people grow, even if your paths eventually lead in different directions.

My Church Family

📅 2023 - Present 📍 Our Church

One of the most meaningful connections in my life is my circle in our church, whom I proudly call my cellmates. They became my second family.

Whenever I feel overwhelmed or discouraged, I know I can always turn to them. They listen, support me, and remind me that I am never alone.

Our leader, Tatay Coste, became like a second father to me. He guided, encouraged, and supported me whenever I needed someone.

Because of them, I became stronger in my faith, more confident, and inspired to encourage others.

💡 Lesson Learned

Family is not only the people related by blood. It is also the people who choose to stay with you.
